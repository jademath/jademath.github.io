I have created **Histogram Studio**, a live page for exploring histograms interactively, as a teaching resource for **MATH 3570: Multivariate Analysis** at Wenzhou-Kean University.

[Open Histogram Studio](/histogram/)

You can generate samples from common distributions, choose the number of observations, or paste your own data. Adjust the bin width and origin with sliders or exact values to see the histogram update immediately, and switch between counts and density.

The page accompanies the histogram discussion in Lecture 1.2. Try keeping the data fixed while changing the bin width or shifting the origin: the same sample can produce quite different-looking histograms.

You can also find the tool under **Live pages** on the [Teaching page](/teaching/).
